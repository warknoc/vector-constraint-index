# Where VCI stands

**It is good, and parts of it are original. How much it matters has not been decided yet.**

## What is strong

**The Delgado Vector Law is the real contribution.** It counts a problem's routes and asks whether they share a constraint, and uses that to decide whether ongoing spending is legitimate. Epidemiology has a narrow version: eradication planners ask whether a disease has an animal reservoir, which is why smallpox could be eradicated and malaria could not. No generalization of that test into a rule for contracts, programs, software, and research is known to the author. That is the part worth citing.

**It turns Shirky into something usable.** The Shirky Principle has been a well-known observation since 2010, but never a test anyone could run. VCI makes it one, with named verdicts and a citation behind each.

**The construction is disciplined.** It is deterministic. It defines its terms so they cannot be quietly redefined. Its escape hatches close once resources are committed. It separates structure from intent. Most frameworks in this space are checklists; this one reaches a verdict.

**It passed its own test in public.** The framework's own flagship case was wrong, and the correction was made and logged before release. Then it was published with an invitation to break it.

## What is not proven yet

**Unvalidated.** Five cases, run by the author, outcomes already known. Until independent evaluators score blind cases and agree, it is a well-built claim, not a proven tool.

**The weak point is judgment at each question.** The routing is deterministic; whether a given answer is "Yes" is not. The blind run is where that gets tested, and where it is most likely to need fixing. See `ROADMAP.md`.

**It enters a crowded field:** the Heilmeier Catechism, logic models, audit criteria, theory-of-change frameworks. VCI has a real edge over them in falsification, lanes, and kill forks, and that edge has to be shown in use, one teardown at a time.

## Rating

- **As a document:** well above what is usually published in this space.
- **As an idea:** one original core, the Vector Law, inside a strong synthesis of established methods.
- **As significance:** undetermined. The Heilmeier Catechism mattered because DARPA used it for fifty years. VCI's significance will come from the blind run and from whether it catches things people care about.

The work that was in the author's control is done. The rest comes from running it on real programs, and from anyone willing to try to break it.
